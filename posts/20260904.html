How all my websites are made

And why AI has never really been part of it

Drum roll please! All of my websites are born before the year that marks the commence of the recent AI boom, namely, 2023. ChatGPT is born in November 2022, and my website was already live in October.

And I'm gonna hold your hand when I say this: things could not have been better then. I genuinely enjoyed spending days roaming on Stack Overflow for the tiniest thing you could be an expert in and become an expert in it. During the early days, the question was: how do I stop writing the same header and the same footer in every HTML file over and over again?

(Un)surprisingly, no one really had an answer other than "well, you should use Jekyll or some other renderer to put everything together." Or maybe I wasn't able to scour the resources hard enough because I was in China. I ended up finding what I wanted on CSDN. Someone gave a JavaScript implementation of an "<include>" tag. Sadly I can't find that post again.

A lot can be said on what "no AI-generated content" means today. When I used that phrase, I meant that every word you read, every button you click, and everything you see on this website is typed by my own hands into a code editor.

However, when I was learning to use some frameworks like Flask that clears the clutter and optimizes the performance of this website, I asked ChatGPT for help.
